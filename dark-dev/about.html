About

I'm Mayur, a front-end developer in Mumbai. I build WordPress sites and Shopify stores that load fast, read clearly, and are easy for the owner to run.

Before I wrote code, I worked as an e-commerce executive: listing products, handling orders, and watching what made shoppers buy or leave. That desk taught me more about websites than any tutorial. I still build from it: the product photo has to be right, the price has to be clear, and checkout can't make anyone think twice.

Since moving into IT I've shipped more than 25 projects, including the eight in my work section, for skincare brands, a dairy, a streetwear label, a Jaipur textile house, a coaching institute, an interiors firm and a real estate developer.

I use AI tools for the routine parts of the job, so I can spend more time on what matters: layout, speed, and the small details that make a site feel finished.
